Scout opens on the Shopify App Store on December 15, 2026. Until then, early access is free: there is nothing to install or pay, and we set Scout up on your store with you. Billing below applies from launch.

These terms are between you, the store owner, and Gestion Emile Chouha inc., a Canadian company, which makes Scout under the name Living Store ("Scout", "we"). By joining early access or installing Scout you agree to them.

The service

Scout invites your shoppers to short voice interviews, through a banner on your store or by email through your own Klaviyo. An AI interviewer talks with them about what they did on your store and why. You get every recording and transcript, the reasons people give, and a discount code goes to each shopper who finishes an interview, for the value you set.

Plans and billing

  • Early access is free. From launch, Scout is billed by Shopify on your Shopify invoice, in US dollars, every 30 days, at the price Shopify shows you before you approve it.
  • Scout starts free: every store gets $10 of usage on us, no card, enough for its first few customer interviews. When it runs out, Scout pauses until you upgrade to a plan. Plans are Starter $49, Growth $129 and Scale $250 a month, each with monthly usage included. Usage beyond your plan is billed by how much Scout uses, on your Shopify invoice, never above the monthly cap you approve.
  • To cancel, uninstall Scout from your Shopify admin; Shopify stops the charge. Switching plans replaces your current plan as soon as you approve the new one on Shopify's screen.
  • We do not refund partial billing periods.
  • A few pilot stores that pay us directly by card are billed through Stripe; they cancel from Usage & billing in the app or by email.

Your responsibilities

  • Tell your shoppers, for example in your store's privacy policy, that you use an AI interviewer and that interviews are recorded.
  • Invite by email only people you are allowed to email.
  • Honour the discount codes Scout issues on your behalf.

The interviewer and its findings

The interviewer is an AI. It can get things wrong, and its summaries are a starting point, not a verdict. You decide what to change in your store; we don't promise any sales result.
