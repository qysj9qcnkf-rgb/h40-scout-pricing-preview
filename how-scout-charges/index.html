How Scout charges: the Grok Bot model (H40, Nov 15 2026)

Emile, Nov 15 2026: "How does Grokbot do the free trial? It's not a free trial. It's based on credit and token consumption... I literally want to do the same thing... I don't even think they have a free package. You can come in for free."

This replaces the 7-day free trial (Nov 14 draft). There is no free trial.

What Grok Bot does (grokbot.com now goes to x.ai/bot, read Nov 15 2026)

  • "Get started for free": the app is free to download and start.
  • Monthly plans, each with usage included: Premium $30, Super $100 ("3.5x Premium usage"), Ultra $200 ("12x Premium usage").
  • "Grok Bot subscriptions come with weekly usage included, with additional usage billed based on token cost."
  • No free trial, no free plan, no free starter credits.

The multipliers are a proposal for Emile to confirm; until then the site says "More usage included" and "The most usage included". They come from Grok Bot's usage per dollar applied to our prices: Super gets 1.05x Premium's usage per dollar, so Growth (2.6x Starter's price) rounds to 3x; Ultra gets 1.8x per dollar, so Scale (5.1x Starter's price) rounds to 9x. How much usage Starter includes is Product's call, set from real cost per interview (not measured yet). Grok Bot doesn't publish its amount either.

What marketing says (same words everywhere)

  • Scout home, Pricing: "Install Scout free. Pick a plan when you want Scout talking to your shoppers. Every plan includes monthly usage, and extra usage is billed by how much Scout uses." Plan cards show Starter $49 "Monthly Scout usage included", Growth $129 "More usage included" and Scale $250 "The most usage included" (the 3x / 9x go on once confirmed). Below them, "How billing works": 1) Install free. 2) Pick a plan. 3) Pay for extra only if you use it.
  • /terms and /support: "Installing Scout is free; nothing is charged until you approve a plan. Plans are Starter $49, Growth $129 and Scale $250 a month, each with monthly usage included. Usage beyond your plan is billed by how much Scout uses, on your Shopify invoice, never above the monthly cap you approve."
  • App Store listing (H27) and creator pitch (H28): free to install, the three plans with usage included, extra usage billed by use. No trial.

What the app does today, and what Product builds

  • Today each Shopify plan is a flat monthly charge with a 7-day trial (lib/shopifybilling.mjs), and no usage allowance or overage.
  • The app already has Shopify usage billing from before the plans: a capped usage line item, an hourly report of usage × markup (appUsageRecordCreate, settleShopify, CAP_USD).
  • Stores that pay directly by card already run on exactly the "credit and token consumption" model: no trial, a $25 prepaid top-up, then every conversation comes off the balance at usage × markup (lib/billing.mjs). The Shopify plans adopt the same metering for usage past the allowance.
  • Until the Product subtask ships, the app still gives 7 trial days on a flat plan, so this pricing must not go live before it.
