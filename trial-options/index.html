Scout free trial: options and pick (H40, Nov 14 2026)

Emile, Nov 14 2026: "Anybody could try. We can have a trial period... It should be like the standard practice for how we onboard people on a trial period."

What exists today (from the code)

  • Shopify App Store installs: every plan (Starter $49, Growth $129, Scale $250) starts with a 7-day free trial, once per store. Shopify holds the charge until the trial ends; uninstalling before then costs nothing. Reinstalling never gives a second trial; switching plans keeps only the days left. (lib/shopifybilling.mjs trialDaysLeft, tested in test/embedded-billing.test.mjs.)
  • Stores that sign up directly with a card (not through Shopify): no trial. The card is charged a $25 credit top-up on day one (lib/billing.mjs, "No trial", Oct 6). This is the gap Product has to close.
  • Not built yet: a reminder before the trial ends, and a trial counter the owner can see outside Settings.

The options

OptionWhat the store seesCost and risk for usFit
A. 7 days free (recommended)"7-day free trial · cancel anytime" on every planLowest: one week of AI usage per trial store. Already built, tested and in every draft (home, /terms, /support, App Store listing)A busy store sees real interviews inside a week. Emile already approved it (Oct 31)

C. 30 days / first month free"First month free"Highest: a full month of AI cost per store, and the first payment is a month awayStrong pull, but pays for many stores that never convert
D. Usage-capped trial (7 days or a set amount of usage, whichever comes first)"7 days free" plus a limitCost per trial is capped. Needs a Shopify cap that the Shopify path doesn't have yet (the old direct trial had one: $20 of usage)Safest on cost, but harder to explain in one line

Cost per trial store: not measured yet; no trial store has run on a paid plan. The only number in the code is the old direct-card trial's cap of $20 of usage (TRIAL.budget_cents). Product's subtask should measure it on the first trial stores, so the 7 vs 14 days call can be made on real cost.

Pick

A: 7 days free on every plan, once per store, the same on both sign-up paths. It is the trial Emile already said yes to, it is live in the app's Shopify billing, and every marketing draft already says it. If trial stores don't see enough interviews in a week, move to B by changing one number and the copy.

What marketing says (same words everywhere)

The live marketing site is Scout (marketing/scout-early-access/site, scoutyourstore.com).

  • Home, new "Pricing" section: Starter $49, Growth $129, Scale $250 a month, each "7-day free trial", then "How your free trial works": 1) Install and pick a plan. 2) 7 days free: switch interviews on, nothing charged. 3) Day 8 Shopify bills your plan; uninstall before then and you pay nothing.
  • The one trial sentence, word for word on home, /terms, /support and the App Store listing (H27), and for the creator pitch (H28): "7-day free trial on your first plan, once per store. Uninstall within 7 days and you pay nothing."
